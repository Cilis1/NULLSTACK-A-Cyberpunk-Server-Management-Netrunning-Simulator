# NULLSTACK design

## Vision
A management simulation about building infrastructure under economic, physical and investigative pressure. The player begins in a noodle shop back room and expands from a single rack into a distributed operation. The interface is the VOIDSHELL workstation, not a view of an avatar. The tone is bureaucratic, analog and dry.

## Player loop
Acquire and configure hardware; allocate workloads; accept contracts or infiltrate targets; maintain power, cooling, security and liquidity; invest proceeds in research and expansion. Heat and failures make growth a question of risk rather than a linear upgrade path. Losses should be costly without automatically ending a campaign.

## Interlocking systems
- **Physical:** circuits, utilization-based power, thermal diffusion, wear and maintenance make capacity conditional on its environment. Facilities have different costs and risk profiles.
- **Logical:** services compete for compute, memory, storage and network quotas; scheduling and failures affect contractual SLAs. Monitoring and patching trade visibility and security against cost and downtime.
- **Net:** targets are generated graphs with differentiated defenses. Actions consume time and add noise; tracing is distinct from alertness. Proxy hops protect identity at a latency cost. Orphaned infrastructure can be salvaged, stripped, used as a hop or absorbed, with ownership and honeypot risks.
- **Economy and heat:** white, grey and black work offer different returns and exposure. Persistent activity patterns can produce investigations even after immediate heat falls. Rent, energy and penalties constrain expansion.
- **Progression and world:** research and discoveries unlock cross-branch capabilities; factions, crew and a distributed-process mystery contextualize decisions. Failure may award legacy progress for a subsequent run.

## Presentation and accessibility
VOIDSHELL offers both graphical views and a command shell issuing the same commands. Interfaces must not rely on color alone. Pause, speed controls, readable text and disabling visual effects are priorities. Audio and CRT effects are optional presentation, not simulation inputs.

## Scope and sequencing
The full brief describes a long-term product, not currently implemented features. Milestones and acceptance criteria are tracked in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). The first deliverable is a reproducible, headless simulation foundation; no claim of a playable campaign or installer is made at M0.
